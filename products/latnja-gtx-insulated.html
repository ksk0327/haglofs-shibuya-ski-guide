About this jacket

Inspired by the Vassi freeride series, the Latnja adds built-in insulation for genuinely cold days — resort skiing, lift-served touring, or anywhere you'd rather not layer a separate midlayer underneath. A waterproof GORE-TEX 2L shell is paired with a lightweight synthetic fill, mapped so warmth is added where you need it most without overheating on the move.

Key features

  • Insulated construction (mapped fill — more warmth in core areas, less where you move the most)
  • Body-mapped insulation placement to reduce overheating during activity
  • Waterproof, breathable GORE-TEX construction
  • Suited to freeride performance as well as everyday resort and all-mountain skiing
