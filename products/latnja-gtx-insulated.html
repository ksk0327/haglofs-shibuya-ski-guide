About this jacket

Inspired by the Vassi freeride series, the Latnja adds built-in insulation for genuinely cold days — resort skiing, lift-served touring, or anywhere you'd rather not layer a separate midlayer underneath. A waterproof GORE-TEX ePE 2-layer shell is paired with a lightweight Mimic SILVER fill, mapped across three zones so warmth is added where you need it most without overheating on the move.

Key features

  • 2-way adjustable, helmet-compatible hood
  • Snow skirt with rubber grip to seal out powder
  • Stand collar with a removable brushed chin guard, plus zippered ventilation
  • Diagonally placed 2-way zip ventilation
  • 2 zippered hand pockets, 1 large chest pocket, 1 ski-pass sleeve pocket
  • Stretch wrist gaiters under adjustable velcro cuffs
  • Adjustable drawcord hem
  • RECCO® reflector — a built-in chip that helps rescuers locate you if buried in an avalanche
