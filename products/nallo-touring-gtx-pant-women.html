About this item

The women's version of Haglöfs' lightweight Nallo Touring GTX pant, built for ski touring rather than the lift line. A light 30-denier GORE-TEX ripstop shell keeps weight down, with a snow gaiter compatible with walk-mode ski boots and Twiceme® safety technology built in.
