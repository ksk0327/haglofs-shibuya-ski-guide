About this jacket

The everyday version of Haglöfs' Vassi freeride series. A 3-layer GORE-TEX shell gives the same waterproof, windproof protection as the Pro II at a more accessible price, in a slightly heavier, more durable 75-denier fabric — a strong all-round choice for resort and backcountry skiing alike.

Key features

  • Large helmet-compatible hood, adjustable from two points
  • Chin guard with laser-cut ventilation flap and soft fleece lining
  • Angled 2-way pit-zip ventilation that stays clear of a backpack
  • 2 hand pockets with gusseted zips
  • 1 zippered chest pocket
  • Goggle wipe
  • Ski-pass pocket on the left upper sleeve
  • Open mesh inner pocket
  • Stretch powder skirt with rubber grip
  • Articulated elbows
  • Wide velcro cuff adjusters
  • Drawcord hem
  • RECCO® reflector — a built-in chip that helps rescuers locate you if buried in an avalanche

Materials & specs

Shell fabric3-layer GORE-TEX, 100% recycled polyester, 75 denier (denier = fabric thickness; higher = tougher, more durable)
MembraneGORE-TEX ePE laminate (a 2-layer waterproof membrane)
Waterproofness28,000mm
Breathability (RET)20 — lower RET = more breathable; 20 is a solid everyday resort/freeride level
