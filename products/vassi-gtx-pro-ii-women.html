About this item

The women's version of Haglöfs' top-of-line Vassi freeride jacket, for skiers who split their time between resort laps and backcountry lines. A 3-layer GORE-TEX Pro shell — Haglöfs' most protective, most breathable waterproof fabric — handles deep snow, high wind and long days without overheating.

Key features

  • 3-way adjustable, helmet-compatible hood with chin guard
  • 2-way slanted zippered pit-zip ventilation
  • 2 zippered front pockets, 2 chest pockets (one with a goggle wipe)
  • Ski-pass pocket on the sleeve
  • 2 open mesh inner pockets
  • Integrated stretch snow skirt with rubber grip
  • Articulated sleeves with velcro-adjustable cuffs
  • Cord-adjustable hem
  • 2-way center-front zipper
  • RECCO® reflector for avalanche rescue search
